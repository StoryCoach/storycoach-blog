## How AI Feedback Works

At StoryCoach, we've trained our AI on the proven frameworks and techniques of master storytellers. When you submit a story for feedback, the AI analyzes it through multiple lenses:

**Structure:** Does your story have a clear beginning, middle, and end? Is there a five-second moment where everything changed?

**Stakes:** What's at risk? Why does this moment matter? Can the audience feel the tension?

**Transformation:** What changed? How are you different because of this experience?

**Delivery:** Is your pacing effective? Are you showing emotion or just describing it? Does your ending resonate?

The result? Specific, actionable feedback that helps you see what's working and what needs refinement—just like a personal coach would provide.

But here's the key difference: You're still in control. The AI doesn't rewrite your story. It doesn't strip away your voice. It simply points out what the masters would notice and suggests how to strengthen your narrative.
